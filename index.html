About Us

Trusted Healthcare Partner in Bilekahalli Since 2009

Anand Multispeciality Hospital has been a cornerstone of healthcare in Bilekahalli, Bengaluru, providing comprehensive medical services with a commitment to excellence. Our hospital is equipped with modern infrastructure and staffed by a team of dedicated professionals who prioritize patient well-being above all.

From routine check-ups to complex surgeries, we offer a wide spectrum of medical services under one roof. Our patient-first approach ensures that every individual receives personalized care tailored to their specific needs.

To make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for every family in our community.

Our Services

Comprehensive Medical Services

We offer a wide range of medical and surgical specialities to cater to all your healthcare needs under one roof.

Cardiology

Complete cardiac care including ECG, Echo, TMT, and interventional procedures with experienced cardiologists.

Orthopaedics

Joint replacement, fracture management, sports injury treatment, and spine surgery with advanced techniques.

Neurology

Diagnosis and treatment of neurological disorders including stroke, epilepsy, and nerve-related conditions.

Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Comprehensive women's health services including prenatal care, high-risk pregnancies, and laparoscopic surgeries.

General Surgery

Minimally invasive and open surgical procedures for hernia, appendicitis, gallbladder, and more.

Ophthalmology

Complete eye care including cataract surgery, LASIK, glaucoma treatment, and retinal procedures.

ENT

Treatment for ear, nose, and throat disorders including sinus surgery, hearing evaluation, and sleep apnea.

General Medicine

Expert diagnosis and management of common illnesses, chronic diseases, lifestyle disorders, and preventive health.

Diagnostics & Lab

NABL-certified laboratory with advanced imaging — X-ray, CT scan, MRI, Ultrasound, and pathology services.

Emergency & Trauma

24/7 fully equipped emergency department with trauma care, ambulance services, and critical care unit.

Pulmonology

Respiratory care including asthma, COPD management, sleep studies, and bronchoscopy procedures.

Dental Care

Comprehensive dental services including root canal, implants, braces, dentures, and cosmetic dentistry.
