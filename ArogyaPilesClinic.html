Our Services

Comprehensive Treatments

Advanced, minimally invasive procedures for all anorectal conditions with quick recovery and lasting results.

Piles Treatment

Advanced laser & rubber band ligation for internal and external piles. Painless procedure with minimal recovery time.

  • Grades I to IV piles
  • Bleeding & non-bleeding
  • No recurrence guarantee

Fissure Treatment

Effective treatment for anal fissures including chronic cases. Medication, botox therapy, and surgical options available.

  • Acute & chronic fissures
  • Botox injection therapy
  • Pain relief in 24 hours

Fistula Treatment

Specialized fistula repair using advanced techniques. VAAFT, seton placement, and fistulotomy procedures.

  • Simple & complex fistula
  • Sphincter-saving techniques
  • Minimal scarring

Pilonidal Sinus

Complete excision and healing of pilonidal sinus with modern surgical methods ensuring minimal downtime.

  • Acute & recurrent cases
  • Minimal blood loss
  • Rapid wound healing

Constipation Care

Comprehensive evaluation and management of chronic constipation with dietary, medical, and procedural interventions.

  • Chronic constipation
  • Biofeedback therapy
  • Long-term relief

Diagnosis & Consultation

Thorough diagnostic evaluation including digital examination, proctoscopy, and personalized treatment planning.

  • Digital rectal exam
  • Proctoscopy
  • Custom treatment plan
